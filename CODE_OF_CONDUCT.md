# Code of conduct

This applies to everyone taking part in RSOcc26 repositories: code, issues,
pull requests, reviews and discussions.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and considerate, in particular when you disagree.
- Review the work, not the person; give concrete, constructive feedback.
- Accept feedback gracefully and assume good intent.
- Help keep discussions focused on the project.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults or discriminatory remarks of any kind.
- Personal attacks or deliberately disruptive behaviour.
- Publishing someone else's private information without their permission.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the organization owners
([@Gregx12](https://github.com/Gregx12), [@jp7316](https://github.com/jp7316)).
Reports are handled confidentially.

## Consequences

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and pull
requests that break this code of conduct, and may restrict access to the
organization for repeated or serious violations.
